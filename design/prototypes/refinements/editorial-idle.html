What changed
  1. One toolbar. Tabs and the icon layout switch share the first row; search, month, year and sort share the second. The layout switch and the count no longer take two rows of their own.
  2. Search has a placeholder ("Search Posts": the post type's own label, translated by WordPress); its label stays for assistive technology.
  3. No Reset button. Active filters appear as chips with one Clear all; when nothing is active there is nothing to clear, so nothing is shown.
  4. Layout switch is two icon buttons with accessible names ("Grid view", "List view") and a filled selected state.
  5. No-image fallback (4th card, or the Appointment card): a soft tinted tile with a monogram badge instead of a flat block. Same tile in both designs.
  6. States: the 2nd card shows hover (border and shadow), the 3rd shows the keyboard focus ring.
