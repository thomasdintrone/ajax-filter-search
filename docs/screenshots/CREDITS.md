# Photo credits for the listing screenshots

The demo site behind the WordPress.org screenshots uses free photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com). The [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) allows free use, including commercial use, without permission or attribution; the credits are listed anyway. The photos appear only in the screenshots (and the two thumbnails inside the "What's new" card, which are crops of those screenshots). They are **not** part of the plugin and are **not stored in this repository**: `node docs/screenshots/fetch-photos.mjs` downloads them from Unsplash's image server into `docs/screenshots/photos/`, which is git-ignored.

All demo organizations, articles and people named in the screenshots are fictional. None of the photos shows a recognizable face, logo or readable sign.
